Field guide
  1. Open a project. Import old KML / SLD99 DXF.
  2. Choose Polygon and tap the map. Watch the live area and OSNAP label.
  3. Shared boundary: pick start and end, inspect the highlighted route, then Use boundary.
  4. Enter land information and Save. Enter required extent and Subdivide.
  5. Draw a crossing line, tap the desired side, check the preview, keep one or both parts.
  6. Save a project backup before leaving the field.

SLD99 grid area is used. GPS and imagery are for sketches; verify control and boundaries before a final survey plan. Unsupported DXF curves are reported, not silently made straight.

App and references are stored on this device. Imagery needs internet; cached tiles may be available offline. Never clear site data without a project backup.
